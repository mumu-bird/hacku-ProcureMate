## HKT 声明文案

ProcureMate addresses HacKU 2026 FinTech Problem Statement 1, “Give a Machine a Wallet – Agentic Commerce”, sponsored by HKT. The prototype supports delegated retail procurement for events, office replenishment and employee onboarding. It demonstrates a complete test transaction, server-enforced spending authorization, a stopped over-budget purchase, and auditable execution records. Product prices and shipping policies are timestamped source snapshots. Current demonstration uses simulated payments; no actual HKT merchant purchase or company wallet connection is claimed.
